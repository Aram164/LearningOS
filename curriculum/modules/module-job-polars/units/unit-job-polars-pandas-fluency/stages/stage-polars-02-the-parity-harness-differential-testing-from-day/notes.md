# Stage Working Notes

## Mental Models

### Why this is Stage 2 and not Stage 15

A contract table written from documentation records what the libraries promise. Only differential execution finds what they actually do — the silent upcast, the unstable tie order, the group key that vanished. The harness has to exist before the stages that generate those findings.

### Cumulative, not per-stage

One harness, grown fourteen times. By the capstone it is a corpus of every divergence you have personally hit, which is the artifact that transfers to a method you have never seen.

### A parity test that cannot fail is decoration

Normalization is how a test stops reporting a difference. Every normalization is therefore a claim that the difference does not matter, and belongs in the policy with a reason beside it.
