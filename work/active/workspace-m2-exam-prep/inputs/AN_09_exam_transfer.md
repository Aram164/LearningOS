## What to produce

1. **Mixed retrieval sets that ignore chapter boundaries.** The exam does not
   announce which node a question belongs to; practice that includes the
   identification step. Build sets from your **error ledger**, not from the
   script's table of contents.
2. **At least two full timed sittings** under real conditions — Ulm, then
   Leipzig, spaced so the first one's repairs land before the second.
3. **Combined M2 mocks.** The Klausur is **one 3-hour paper covering SaD *and*
   Analysis for one grade.** Practising the halves separately never rehearses the
   thing that actually goes wrong: switching register mid-paper and allocating
   time across two subjects. **At least one full combined mock is
   non-negotiable.**
4. **The final ledger pass.** By now the ledger has entries from all nine nodes.
   Sort by class — `definition · theorem-condition · method-choice · algebra ·
   time` — and let the largest class choose the last sessions. A `time` class
   that dominates means a pacing problem, not a knowledge problem, and is fixed
   differently.

## How to self-test

This node *is* the test. The checks are about performance, not recall:

- [ ] A timed sitting completed **within its time limit**, not merely completed.
- [ ] Mixed sets where you identify the required tool before computing.
- [ ] One combined SaD + Analysis mock at full length.
- [ ] Ledger classes trending down between sittings — the actual evidence of
      progress.

**Done when** the timing holds and the error ledger stops producing new entries
in classes you thought were closed.

## Scheduling — the part that decides whether this node happens

Per COORDINATION.md: AML sits first, M2 sits **nine days later**, and Phase C is
*"a finish, not a start."* Set against 46 exam-critical hours of Analysis:

- **AN.0–AN.G (2,220 min ≈ 37 h) must be substantially done *before* the AML
  sitting.** They are the part that can run in parallel with AML, because they
  are block-building and tolerate interruption.
- **AN.X (540 min ≈ 9 h) is what Phase C is for.** It needs contiguous, unbroken
  sessions — a timed sitting cannot be done in twenty-minute gaps.
- If AN.0–AN.G slip past the AML sitting, **AN.X is what gets sacrificed**, and
  the track arrives at a combined Klausur having never rehearsed the combined
  format. That is the specific failure this plan exists to prevent.

## Traps

- **Solving Ulm/Leipzig early "just to see."** It permanently destroys the only
  fresh timed material you have. The routes reserved them for a reason.
- **Practising the two M2 halves separately, always.** One paper, one grade, one
  three-hour block — rehearse it that way at least once.
- **Untimed "mixed practice."** Without the clock this collapses into revision,
  which is AN.0–AN.G's job, already done.
- **Adding new sources here.** Anything you haven't used by now costs orientation
  time. Go back to the book that already worked for that node.
- **Treating 540 minutes as flexible.** It is the largest block in the track. It
  is not slack.
